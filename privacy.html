Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Methodist Panel Tracker and GridIQ Pro iOS apps (the "Apps"). Your privacy matters, and these Apps are designed to keep your data in your hands.

Information We Collect

The Apps do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information to any third party. There are no analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or hidden data collection. Any notes, panel records, and photos you create stay on your device.

Data Storage

  • All notes and images are stored locally on your device.
  • If you enable iCloud, your data may be backed up and synced across your own devices through your personal Apple iCloud account. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and is not accessible to us.

Camera & Photos

The Apps may request access to your camera or photo library so you can attach images to notes. These photos are used solely within the App and are stored locally (or in your own iCloud). They are never uploaded to our servers — we don't operate any.

Third-Party Services

The Apps do not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or data-sharing services. The only external service involved is Apple's iCloud, and only if you choose to enable it.

Children's Privacy

The Apps are intended for professional and general audiences and do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at dholt25@yahoo.com.
