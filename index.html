Support

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from the field.

How do I export panel notes?
Tap the export icon in the top-right corner of a panel and select PDF. The app builds a formatted document you can share or save.
Can I attach images to notes?
Yes. Tap Select Image in the note section to attach field photos directly to a panel record.
Are my notes synced across devices?
Yes — as long as iCloud Key-Value storage is enabled on your devices, notes sync automatically between them.
Where is my data stored?
All notes and images are saved locally on your device and optionally backed up with iCloud, so they stay available even without a connection.
When is GridIQ launching?
Soon-ish. We promise to flip the breaker on it when it's ready — drop your email in the GridIQ section and you'll be the first to know.
