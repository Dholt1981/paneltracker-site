Frequently asked questions

The quick answers. Need more? Head to the support page.

How do I export panel notes?
Tap the export icon 📤 in the top-right corner and select PDF.
Can I attach images to notes?
Yes — tap "Select Image" in the note section to attach field photos.
Are my notes synced across devices?
Yes, if iCloud Key-Value storage is enabled on your devices.
Where is my data stored?
All notes and images are saved locally and optionally backed up with iCloud.
